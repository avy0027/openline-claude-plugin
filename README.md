# Openline

Openline is an AI frontdesk for Claude. It gives Claude a phone number and an email inbox, and calls, SMS, and emails share the same memory. Ask Claude to place a call, send a text, or send an email, and to answer your front desk, take messages, book a table, schedule an appointment, or follow up with someone.

## Use it

Install the plugin, then open its Connectors tab and connect Openline. Sign-in happens on Openline's page in the browser. After you sign in, an inbox and a phone number are ready.

Try one of these:

- Send a short email from my Openline inbox.
- What phone number did Openline give me?
- Call this number and ask whether they have a table tonight.

Claude uses the Openline connector for mail, calls, and texts, and only when you ask.

## Data

The plugin sends the content of emails, texts, and calls, along with the phone numbers and email addresses you name, to your Openline account on Scalar Field's servers at staging.openline.scalarfield.io. It stores nothing itself and sends that data nowhere else.

You can review calls, email, and SMS at https://staging.openline.scalarfield.io/site/dashboard.
